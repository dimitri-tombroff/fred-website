**Fred provides an open-source, collaborative and governed environment to work with data, use tools and carry out AI-assisted business workflows.**

It offers ready-to-use features that teams can extend with capabilities and applications tailored to their business. Weaver builds on this foundation: bringing people, data and services together in governed spaces, with a polished user experience and execution that keeps complexity and resource use under control.

The question guiding our roadmap and comparison with other platforms is: **how can several people and agents contribute to the same project over time, then consolidate and validate their work?**
